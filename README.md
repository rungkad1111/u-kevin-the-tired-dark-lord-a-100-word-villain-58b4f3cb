A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — a dark lord who wrote the curse that keeps his mountain standing, only to find the small print keeps him. Four centuries of Tuesdays, the same duel, the same speech, and the kettle has gone cold again.

## Entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it:

```sh
node main.ts
```

## The monologue

> Right. So. You have climbed six hundred steps, past the bone choir and the toll gate I had painted, and here we are. The sword, the shouting, the whole performance. Look, I wrote the curse that keeps this mountain standing, and the small print says the mountain keeps me. Four centuries of Tuesdays, the same duel, the same speech, and the kettle has gone cold again. Take the crown. Take the vault, the ravens, the haunted ledger, all of it. Just, when you sit on the throne, love, do me one kindness. Lose the key. I have earned one afternoon.
